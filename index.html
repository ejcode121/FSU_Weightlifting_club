The meet is Apr 15 to 18, 2027, so Nov 2026 through Apr 2027 is 6 months. The plan assumes you work shifts through the last month, and April ends when you leave.

The trip

Arrive two days before your lifters compete, take a rest day, then compete. Pick the competition day to see your dates.

USAW entry fees per lifter, from its August 2026 announcement: $145 early bird through Mar 4, 2027, $175 regular through Mar 18, and $375 late through Apr 1 (2:00 p.m. MT). Registration opens Jan 1, 2027. Paying early saves $30 each, $540 for 18. The default is the $175 regular fee. USAW membership is not included.

    Airbnbs near the venue

    The venue is the Greater Richmond Convention Center. Enter each listing's price breakdown and its distance (check it in Maps). Every stay gets a grade from A to D for distance and cost per person. We work out how many places 18 people need.

    How grades work

    Price makes up the rest of the grade. A score of 75 or more is an A, 55 or more a B, 35 or more a C, and anything lower a D. A listing with no distance can't be graded.

    Getting there

    Rental vans, a charter bus, or flights. A red-eye home, leaving the night of the meet, needs a professional driver or a flight, so vans stay the extra night.

    Every combination

    Team totals with the cost per person, compared with your goal. Charter bus and flights appear twice: the full stay-over and a red-eye with one fewer night. For a red-eye, ask the host about late check-out on meet day. Tap a combination to use it.
